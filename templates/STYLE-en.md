# Style sheet

Every spelling and usage decision, recorded once so all chapters agree. Read it before any edit.
When a report or a language pass settles a new question, add the decision here with its date.

The entries below are examples. Delete what does not fit the book and add the author's own.

## Spelling
- Dictionary and variety (for example US spelling, Merriam-Webster), except inside quotations.
- Numbers: words up to one hundred, figures above; years and dates always in figures.

## Names and foreign terms
- One spelling for every name and transliterated term across the book.
- A technical term gets a plain explanation at first use.
- Book titles: in italics; the original title in parentheses at first mention of a translation.

## Dashes and punctuation
- Decide the dash style once (spaced en dash, or closed em dash) and how often to use it.
- Serial comma: yes or no, decided here.

## The author's words (do not correct)
- Words, idioms and turns of phrase the author uses on purpose, even unusual ones. Before
  correcting an unusual phrase, check this list.

## Cross-references
- Refer to another chapter by its name ("in the chapter on watering"), not by its number or
  by "the previous chapter": numbering changes, and positional references break silently.

## Decisions (with dates)
- YYYY-MM-DD: <the decision, and what prompted it>
